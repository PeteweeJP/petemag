Working closely with Facebook, we created one of the first Facebook Canvas ads in the category, and then a second.

The first promoted the range of breakfasts available only at IHOP, pairing food with moments big and small. The second promoted IHOP's fresh fruit offerings as French toast or crepes, mostly in video, with the price point as a traffic driver. Both ended with directions to the nearest IHOP.
