**Role:** UX, Creative Direction

Heart disease kills more women than all cancers combined. For three years, as an art director at Edelman for the American Heart Association, I helped develop Go Red for Women, Choose to Move and Start.

Go Red for Women became a national movement urging women to take charge of their heart health. Awareness among women rose from about 34% to 54% after launch. A design system, brand guidelines and component library were developed alongside.
