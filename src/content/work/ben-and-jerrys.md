## Executive Summary
During my tenure at Edelman, I served as the Art Director and UX Lead across Ben & Jerry's digital ecosystem. The goal was to elevate the brand's iconic personality through engaging, interactive, and playful digital touchpoints that captured consumer attention and drove brand affinity.

My responsibilities spanned concepting and executing end-to-end digital activations, including custom website experience assets, interactive gamified rich media ad units, Facebook social campaigns, and email marketing blasts. Additionally, I led proactive creative exploratory concepts that pushed the boundaries of Ben & Jerry's digital brand presence.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. Gamified Rich Media & Social Campaigns

- **Interactive Ad Units:** Designed engaging, playable rich media ad experiences that turned passive impressions into active gameplay for users.
- **Social Media Advertising:** Created targeted Facebook ad campaign assets aligned with Ben & Jerry's signature tone and visual identity.

### B. Digital Platform & Email Ecosystem

- **Website Experience Assets:** Designed responsive visual assets and UI elements tailored for the Ben & Jerry's core web experience.
- **Email Marketing:** Developed promotional email blast layouts optimized for high engagement and brand retention.
- **Creative Exploratories:** Concepted forward-thinking, unused digital campaign frameworks to explore new modes of brand interaction.

## Workflows & Governance
- **Brand Playfulness & Authenticity:** Maintained Ben & Jerry's distinct voice, bold typography, and visual charm across every digital touchpoint.
- **Gamified UX Optimization:** Designed intuitive micro-interactions and gameplay mechanics within rich media display constraints to maximize user participation.
