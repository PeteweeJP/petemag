## Enterprise Web Redesign & Lead Generation Funnel Strategy

**Role:** Creative Director<br>
**Core Domain:** UX Strategy, UI Design, Content Storytelling, Global Design Systems, Lead Generation Funnels<br>
**User Journey Scope:** Prospect to Lead Gen

## Executive Summary

Led creative direction, UX strategy, and UI design teams to execute a comprehensive site redesign for Comcast Business Enterprise. Orchestrated cross-functional alignment across product, marketing, engineering, copywriting, research, and content build teams to pivot the enterprise web experience toward high-converting lead generation, richer product storytelling, scalable design systems, and targeted marketing campaign integration.

## Strategic Responsibilities & Execution

### 1. Enterprise Product Storytelling & Content Redesign

- Re-architected page narrative structures across the enterprise site to clearly articulate complex B2B products, managed services, and industry solutions.
- Collaborated with UX research and copywriting teams to transform technical product specifications into benefit-driven, digestible user journeys that guide prospective enterprise buyers.

### 2. Cross-Functional Execution, Design Systems & Component Build

- Directed multidisciplinary teams (UX/UI designers, researchers, copywriters, developers, and content build specialists) within an agile deployment model.
- Led global design system initiatives, overseeing the conceptualization, design, and technical build of scalable UI components to streamline site updates and maintain enterprise brand consistency.

### 3. Public Sector & Campaign Ecosystem Integration

- Built tailored web experiences dedicated to the Public Sector segment, addressing regulatory and specialized organizational requirements.
- Led creative design for major integrated marketing campaigns, including PGA Golf sponsorship integrations and thematic initiatives such as Cybersecurity Awareness Month.

### 4. Lead Generation & Gated Content Strategy

- Designed high-converting lead generation funnels across enterprise product lines, balancing friction-free discovery with strategic content gating.
- Partnered with marketing and research teams to define gated asset placement (e.g., whitepapers, solution briefs, case studies) to capture high-intent B2B sales leads.
