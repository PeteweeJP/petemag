## Mobile Design Leadership & Web Ecosystem Optimization

**Role:** Creative Director<br>
**Core Domain:** UX/UI Design, Creative Direction, Digital Strategy, Design Systems<br>
**User Journey Scope:** Full Funnel (Learn → Buy → Get)

## 1. Executive Summary

As Creative Director, I led cross-functional UX and UI strategy and execution across the full digital web ecosystem for Comcast Business Mobile. Overseeing the end-to-end user experience—spanning discovery, conversion, e-commerce checkout, and onboarding—I directed cross-functional collaboration with sales, marketing, product, development, and engineering teams.

Through strategic discovery, user testing, A/B experimentation, component creation, and global design system integration, I solved complex enterprise business problems while maintaining long-term site health and scalability.

## 3. Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact

### A. Full-Funnel Web Experience (Learn → Buy → Get)

- **Strategy & Discovery:** Synthesized requirements from sales, product, and marketing into clear strategic direction, user flows, wireframes, and production-ready designs.
- **Campaign & Offer Integration:** Oversaw rapid-turnaround and seasonal marketing campaigns, ensuring dynamic offer structures integrated seamlessly into the website architecture without creating UI debt.
- **Site Maintenance & Continuity:** Maintained site stability and brand coherence across multi-channel campaign releases and product launches.

### B. Global Design System & Component Architecture

- **System Integration:** Enforced visual and interaction standards using a unified design system across every touchpoint of the mobile digital portfolio.
- **Scalable UI Components:** Partnered directly with front-end engineers and web developers to create modular, reusable UI components that accelerated time-to-market for marketing initiatives.

### C. Data-Driven UX Optimization & Testing

- **User Testing & Validation:** Led usability studies to uncover user friction points across key conversion pathways.
- **A/B Testing Framework:** Implemented structured multivariate and A/B design experiments to validate layout changes, call-to-action (CTA) optimizations, and checkout flow enhancements prior to full deployment.
