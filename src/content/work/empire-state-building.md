## Empire State Building Digital Redesign

**Roles:** Lead Digital Strategist, UX Designer & Art Director<br>
**Core Deliverables:** Global E-Commerce Web Platform, Design System, Brand Guidelines

## Overview & Objectives

Led the end-to-end digital redesign and brand evolution for the Empire State Building's web ecosystem to serve three core audiences:

- **Global Observatory Tourists:** Direct, high-conversion ticket sales engine (multi-currency/language).
- **NYC Visitors:** Comprehensive tourist package, interactive exhibit guide, and event directory.
- **Commercial Tenants:** Direct B2B portal for corporate leasing and business inquiries.

## The Problem vs. Solution

| Legacy Issues | Redesign Solutions |
| --- | --- |
| **Fragmented Journeys:** B2C tourism and B2B commercial content competed for attention. | **Segmented IA:** Dedicated user paths for Observatory Visitors, Tourists, and B2B Tenants. |
| **High Ticket Drop-Off:** Complex, friction-heavy purchasing flow for global users. | **Streamlined Funnel:** Frictionless 3-step ticket checkout with clear ticket tier comparisons. |
| **Brand Dilution:** Inconsistent visual elements and unstandardized layouts across sub-pages. | **Unified Design System:** Modernized Art Deco design language with reusable UI components. |

## Key Strategic Pillars

### 1. Global E-Commerce Hub

- Simplified checkout for Standard, Express, Sunset, and VIP packages.
- On-site mobile optimization for instant ticket access, live wait times, and audio tours.

### 2. Destination & Experience Guide

- Interactive exhibit previews, dining highlights, and curated NYC tourist bundles.
- Content strategy tailored for international travel planning.

### 3. Modular Design System & Art Direction

- **Visual Identity:** Elevated typography and sleek layouts paired with classic Art Deco heritage.
- **UI Kit:** Scalable design system including grid structures, accessible color palettes, and component guidelines for future iterations.

## Deliverables Summary

- **Strategy:** User personas, conversion flows, and IA architecture map.
- **UX/UI:** Interactive prototypes, wireframes, and multi-iteration UI designs.
- **Art Direction:** Brand guidelines, design system documentation, and image direction.

## Key Outcomes

- **Unified Digital Platform:** Seamlessly balanced heavy B2C tourism traffic with high-value B2B leasing leads.
- **Scalable Framework:** Established design guidelines that accelerated future sub-brand and mobile features.
