## Executive Summary
To attract a broader user base of active, engaged video reviewers and elevate its enterprise market presence, Expo TV initiated a comprehensive brand and digital platform overhaul. The strategic objective was to position Expo TV as the central authority facilitating authentic communication and consumer-generated reviews between everyday consumers and leading brands.

As the sole Creative Director, I executed the end-to-end rebranding program hands-on across all touchpoints. I designed the foundational brand identity system—including logo concepts that merged camera imagery with user-to-brand dialogue—engineered the complete website redesign, authored comprehensive brand guidelines, and developed marketing collateral and executive presentation decks to establish long-term brand governance.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. Logo Concepting & Visual Identity Architecture

- **Camera Eye & Conversation Metaphor:** Designed two foundational logo concepts merging the visual elements of a camera lens ("Camera Eye") with the flow of authentic user-to-brand conversation.
- **Central Hub Positioning:** Architected the logo mark to position Expo TV visually as the central nexus connecting consumer interaction, brand engagement, and authentic product reviews.
- **Expanded Palette & Authoritative Typography:** Established a friendly yet authoritative color palette and bold typography hierarchy designed to appeal equally to everyday consumers and enterprise brand clients.

### B. Dual-Audience Website Overhaul & Digital Ecosystem

- **Targeted User Journeys:** Architected distinct digital site paths tailored specifically for two primary audiences:
  1. **Consumer & Reviewer Journey:** Guided everyday users through the benefits of creating video reviews, participating in brand campaigns, and engaging with the community.
  2. **Enterprise Brand Journey:** Educated brand partners on how Expo TV facilitates authentic consumer reviews and honest communication to drive product sales.
- **Photoshop UI Comps & Asset Systems:** Designed high-fidelity desktop and mobile UI comps in Photoshop, structuring scalable templates and layout modules for site-wide execution.

### C. Master Brand Guidelines & Collateral Ecosystem

- **InDesign Brand Guidelines:** Authored comprehensive brand books in InDesign detailing logo usage, clear space, color palettes, typography, and multi-channel asset rules.
- **Marketing Collateral & Presentation Decks:** Designed sales enablement collateral, print materials, and executive PPT/InDesign pitch decks to extend the new brand identity across all corporate and consumer touchpoints.

## Workflows & Governance
- **100% Solo Hands-On Execution:** Independently created all design strategy, visual identity explorations, platform UI layouts, brand guideline books, and sales collateral without delegation.
- **Photoshop & InDesign Mastery:** Leveraged traditional Adobe Creative Cloud workflows to build pixel-precise UI comps, brand specification manuals, and print-ready collateral.
- **Systemic Governance:** Standardized design tokens, visual rules, and collateral assets to ensure long-term brand consistency across future platform updates and enterprise marketing campaigns.
