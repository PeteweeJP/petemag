## Executive Summary
To evolve beyond its legacy identity as Expo.tv and pivot toward a modern, creator-led media network, the company initiated a complete brand transformation into **gen.video**. The strategic objective was twofold: attract premium consumer brands looking for authentic influencer partnerships, while streamlining the creator network to focus on their most engaged, high-quality content aggregators.

As the sole hands-on designer across Strategy, UX, and Creative Direction, I executed the end-to-end rebranding program. I created the original brand identity system—including logo design, icon architecture, color palette, and typography—and built dual-audience web experiences designed to educate both brand marketers and content creators. I authored the complete brand guidelines in InDesign and built modular UI component libraries and developer redlines in Photoshop to drive engineering execution.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. Visual Identity & Brand System Creation

- **Icon & Logo Architecture:** Designed the primary gen.video logo and conversation-inspired icon. The icon visually encapsulates ongoing dialogue—representing the broader marketplace conversation made up of individual creator engagements with brands.
- **Warm & Approachable Brand Personality:** Selected a friendly, warm color palette and approachable typography to establish an inviting tone for creators while maintaining professional credibility for enterprise brand clients.
- **InDesign Brand Guidelines:** Authored comprehensive brand guidelines detailing logo usage, clear space, color systems, typography hierarchies, and multi-channel visual application rules.

### B. Dual-Audience Web Experience (`gen.video`)

- **Targeted User Journeys:** Architected distinct digital site paths tailored specifically for two primary audiences:
  1. **Brand & Marketer Journey:** Educated potential clients on the value of honest product reviews and authentic communication strategies to drive sales.
  2. **Creator & Community Journey:** Guided high-engagement video content aggregators through the benefits of joining the gen.video network.
- **Photoshop Component Library & Redline Delivery:** Built structured UI component libraries and responsive web comps in Photoshop, delivering detailed developer redlines and requirement specifications to ensure design fidelity during frontend buildout.

## Workflows & Governance
- **100% Solo Hands-On Design Execution:** Personally designed all visual assets, logo mark iterations, web UI layouts, and brand specification books without external design agency support.
- **Developer Hand-off & Production Specs:** Prepared comprehensive redline documentation, pixel-precise asset specs, and layout rules in Photoshop to streamline engineering implementation.
- **Strategic Rebranding Alignment:** Successfully shifted market perception from Expo.tv to gen.video, establishing the network as a premier hub for authentic brand-creator video partnerships.
