**Role:** Strategy, UX, Creative Direction

Expo.tv rebranded as gen.video to attract new brands and focus on its most engaged creators.

The work covered a new logo and icon, symbolizing the conversation between users and brands, a playful typeface and a warm palette. A new website explained the community and how brands can use honest product reviews.

A design system, brand guidelines and component library were developed alongside.
