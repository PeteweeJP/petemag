## Executive Summary
To launch IHOP's major brand platform transition from "Come together over breakfast at IHOP" to "Eat up every moment," IHOP required a multi-channel campaign celebrating the meaningful everyday moments—both big and small—that happen inside their restaurants.

As Creative Lead, I directed the creative execution and lead-designed the primary campaign tactics while guiding a team of designers to build the supporting digital ecosystem. I concepted, storyboarded, designed, and presented the campaign to client leadership, while also helping direct the on-set 360° video shoot featuring commercial talent. The integrated campaign encompassed an Instagram contest (`#IHOPmoment`), a supporting web voting hub and email nurture sequence, interactive 360° video experiences, and a menu "Moments Generator" that paired IHOP menu items with relatable daily moments across IHOP.com.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. `#IHOPmoment` Instagram Contest & Digital Voting Hub

- **Social Contest Architecture:** Concepted and executed the Instagram-first contest framework where guests shared photos and stories capturing in-restaurant moments using `#IHOPmoment`.
- **Contest Microsite & Daily Voting UX:** Designed user flows and UI layouts in Photoshop for a dedicated campaign web portal where community members submitted entries and returned daily to vote for their favorite contestants.
- **Email Campaign Ecosystem:** Created targeted email blast templates and redline specs in InDesign to drive registered users back to the platform for daily engagement.

### B. Immersive 360° Video Experience

- **Concepting & Storyboarding:** Authored detailed visual storyboards and interactive concepts framing the 360° video narrative featuring talent from national TV commercials.
- **On-Set Production & Client Pitch:** Presented creative direction directly to IHOP leadership and assisted on-set during filming to ensure visual and narrative alignment across Facebook, YouTube, and digital site players.

### C. Menu "Moments Generator" & IHOP.com Integration

- **Dynamic Pairing Engine:** Designed an interactive "Moments Generator" matching everyday life moments with specific IHOP menu items.
- **Core Site Menu Redesign:** Updated IHOP.com menu pages to contextualize food offerings with curated moment pairings, driving emotional connection directly at the point of order consideration.

## Workflows & Governance
- **Hybrid Hands-On & Leadership Model:** Executed core creative concepts, storyboards, and primary layout comps hands-on while providing daily design direction to a team of designers.
- **Production Spec Hand-off:** Built structured layout redlines, visual asset libraries, and UI component decks using Photoshop and InDesign to guide engineering hand-off.
- **Brand Platform Alignment:** Successfully translated a high-level tagline repositioning into an interactive, multi-touchpoint digital experience that drove sustained social user participation.
