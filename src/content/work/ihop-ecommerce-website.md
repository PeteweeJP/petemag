## IHOP Ecommerce Redesign (order.ihop.com)

**Role:** Strategy, UX, Creative Lead<br>
**Core Domain:** E-Commerce Architecture, Design Systems & Component Governance, Mobile-First Web Ordering, Agile UI Engineering Hand-off<br>
**User Journey Scope:** Full Funnel (Market Interception → Location Discovery → Menu Customization → Frictionless Checkout / Fulfillment Choice)

## 1. Executive Summary

Faced with an increasingly crowded restaurant marketplace and shifting consumer dining habits, IHOP sought to expand revenue channels beyond traditional dine-in sales by launching a dedicated online ordering ecosystem (**order.ihop.com**).

As Strategy, UX, and Creative Lead, I directed a team of three designers while remaining actively hands-on in crafting key UI comps, interactive prototypes, and the foundational brand book and design system. We conducted stakeholder interviews and data analytics reviews to architect a fully integrated **IHOP-n-Go** e-commerce experience.

The resulting web application seamlessly integrated with fresh brand assets and new menu photography, empowering users to effortlessly discover nearby restaurant locations, customize complex IHOP-n-Go menu items, select preferred fulfillment methods (pickup vs. delivery), and pay online or in-store—establishing a scalable digital foundation backed by a robust component library and spec system.

## 3. Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact

### A. Dedicated E-Commerce Platform Architecture (`order.ihop.com`)

- **Visual Integration & New Menu Photography:** Integrated high-resolution, [new menu photography](ihop-menu-photoshoot) across ordering flows and item customizer screens to elevate appetite appeal and drive higher ticket sizes.
- **Intelligent Store Locator & Fulfillment Engine:** Designed streamlined user flows letting customers automatically locate the nearest IHOP restaurant and select between pickup or delivery options prior to browsing the menu.
- **Complex Item Customization UI:** Built flexible UI wireframes and high-fidelity comps capable of handling highly customizable meal orders (e.g., pancake stacks, sides, toppings, dietary modifications) without overloading the user experience.
- **Flexible Checkout Integration:** Integrated frictionless payment UI flows accommodating both online transactions and pay-at-store checkout paths.

### B. Scalable Design System & Developer Hand-off

- **Brand Book & Component Library:** Authored comprehensive brand guidelines, UI kits, and reusable component libraries to enforce visual consistency across all digital touchpoints.
- **UI Engineering Delivery:** Established standardized redline spec sheets and UI documentation patterns, directing three designers to deliver production-ready assets seamlessly to development teams.

## 4. Workflows & Execution Standards

- **Creative Leadership & Hands-On Execution:** Balanced team management (leading 3 designers) with direct contribution to high-impact creative deliverables, including interactive prototypes and master brand books.
- **Visual Asset Harmony:** Art directed UI components specifically optimized to showcase the newly updated menu photography across mobile and desktop viewport sizes.
- **Systemic UI Standardization:** Structured design tokens, component libraries, and visual guidelines to ensure long-term design governance and ease of future platform expansion.
