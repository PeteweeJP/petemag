## IHOP Fresh Market Campaign

**Role:** Creative Lead<br>
**Core Domain:** Creative Direction, Campaign Strategy, Digital Ecosystems, Location-Based Marketing<br>
**User Journey Scope:** Full Funnel (Awareness → Engagement → Drive-to-Store)

## 1. Executive Summary

As Creative Lead, I directed the digital creative strategy and execution for the product launch of IHOP's **Fresh Market** Limited Time Offer (LTO). Marking a major strategic milestone for the brand, the campaign introduced a dedicated **price point for the first time in IHOP's history** to lower the barrier to trial and drive immediate foot traffic into physical restaurant locations.

Overseeing the end-to-end digital experience, I led the creation of an integrated campaign centered on intense appetite appeal and ingredient uniqueness. By pairing a complete web ecosystem overhaul with location-gated social activations, rich media publisher placements, and drive-to-store navigation ads, the campaign successfully bridged digital engagement with physical store visits.

## 3. Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact

### A. Web Ecosystem & Full-Funnel Digital Refresh

- **Homepage & Landing Page Redesign:** Overhauled IHOP's core web presence to lead with mouthwatering appetite appeal, highlighting the freshness and quality of the Fresh Market ingredients alongside the new price point messaging.
- **Targeted Email Campaign:** Designed promotional email series deployed to IHOP's subscriber base, driving early awareness and highlighting value directly to loyal guests.

### B. In-Restaurant Geo-Gating & Social Innovation

- **In-Restaurant Snapchat Filters:** Developed custom Snapchat Geofilters promoting both the Fresh Market LTO and IHOP's core equities that could **only be unlocked inside an IHOP restaurant**, creating a direct digital incentive for in-person visits.
- **Multi-Platform Social Creative:** Directed the creation of native social assets including Instagram Snap Ads, custom stickers, and Twitter GIFs designed to capture feed attention through vibrant visual storytelling.

### C. Contextual Media & Drive-to-Store Execution

- **High-Impact Publisher Placements:** Produced rich media banner campaigns deployed across premium digital properties, including **CBS Sports**, to drive broad reach and appetite appeal.
- **Waze Location-Based Advertising:** Integrated drive-to-store mobile advertising on **Waze** to reach drivers in proximity to IHOP locations, capturing high-intent consumers actively on the road.

## 4. Workflows & Cross-Functional Governance

- **Strategic Briefing & Positioning:** Translated IHOP's historic first price point directive and LTO product goals into a high-impact creative direction.
- **Digital Ecosystem Redesign:** Led the visual and structural redesign of homepage and landing page touchpoints to balance brand equity with promotional value.
- **Social & Location Asset Production:** Directed creative teams across multiple social formats (Snapchat, Instagram, Twitter) and location-based platforms (Waze).
- **Media & Engineering Handoffs:** Partnered with paid media and web development teams to deploy rich media banners and execute location-gated digital triggers.
- **In-Market Execution & Performance Monitoring:** Evaluated performance across digital touchpoints to ensure appetite appeal and drive-to-store mechanics performed effectively.
