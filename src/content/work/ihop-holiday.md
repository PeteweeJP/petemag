## IHOP — Holiday Celebrations Campaign

**Role:** Creative Lead (Digital Strategy, Art Direction, UX/UI, Social & Media Integration)<br>
**Core Domain:** Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) Marketing, Seasonal LTO Activation, Multi-Platform Digital & Social Strategy<br>
**User Journey Scope:** Full Funnel (Mass Awareness & Social Hype → Location-Based Intent & Navigation → Direct Store Traffic & Conversion)

## 1. Executive Summary

As Creative Lead for IHOP's national **Holiday Celebrations** campaign, I directed the digital creative strategy, visual execution, and social integration to drive foot traffic into restaurants during the key holiday season. The campaign centered on promoting three limited-time offer (LTO) seasonal pancake varieties alongside a holiday turkey dinner, driving appetite appeal by emphasizing unique seasonal ingredients and festive menu items.

Building upon IHOP’s digital design systems and tactical execution frameworks, I led the creation of an end-to-end digital ecosystem. This spanned website redesign modules, targeted sub-pages (including dedicated Breakfastarian messaging and gift card promotions), high-profile rich media display ads, geo-targeted mobile activations, custom Snapchat filters, custom Twitter/X GIF content, email marketing, and a live-streamed Facebook event inside an IHOP restaurant.

## 3. Key Initiatives & Strategic Execution

### A. Digital Ecosystem & Web Touchpoints

- **Homepage & LTO Information Pages:** Designed high-converting homepage modules and dedicated campaign pages highlighting ingredient photography, seasonal appetite appeal, and secondary messaging such as holiday gift cards.
- **Breakfastarian Targeted Content:** Created a specialized landing page tailored to IHOP’s core enthusiast fan base ("Breakfastarians"), celebrating all-day breakfast culture during the holidays.
- **Email Marketing Integration:** Designed promotional email templates highlighting the limited-time holiday menu to drive repeat visits and gift card purchases.

### B. Social, Live-Stream & Video Strategy

- **In-Restaurant Facebook Live Event:** Directed digital content for a live broadcast filmed inside an active IHOP restaurant, driving real-time social engagement during the holiday season.
- **YouTube & Social Digital Video:** Developed promotional video assets distributed across Facebook and YouTube to showcase holiday menu items and drive campaign awareness.
- **24-Hour #BreakfastFriday Twitter Activation:** Created and deployed 24 distinct GIFs—releasing one per hour on Black Friday—to capture holiday shoppers seeking early morning and late-night fueling.

### C. Geo-Targeted & Dynamic In-Store Interception

- **Location-Locked Snapchat Filters:** Designed custom, geo-fenced Snapchat filters that could only be unlocked when guests were physically inside an IHOP restaurant, driving social sharing and user-generated content.
- **Waze Navigation Partnership:** Integrated custom branded location markers and contextual ads on Waze to intercept drivers and redirect holiday travelers into nearby IHOP locations.
- **High-Impact Rich Media Display:** Designed dynamic rich media banner ads running across premium placements, including CBS Sports, capturing high-volume holiday sports viewer traffic.

## 4. Cross-Functional Leadership & Tactical Execution

- **Digital Alignment:** Leveraged established IHOP digital campaign patterns and visual standards to ensure brand consistency across web, social, and mobile environments.
- **Multi-Agency & Platform Coordination:** Orchestrated creative asset execution across social platforms (Facebook, YouTube, Snapchat, Twitter/X), media partners (CBS Sports), and navigational apps (Waze).
- **Drive-to-Store Focus:** Aligned all visual assets, messaging hierarchy, and interactive elements toward a single primary KPI: driving foot traffic and appetite appeal during the high-volume holiday sales window.
