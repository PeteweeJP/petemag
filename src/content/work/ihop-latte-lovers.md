## Executive Summary
To launch IHOP's new Limited Time Offer (LTO)—Latte Lover's Pancakes—IHOP required a high-impact multi-channel campaign designed to drive in-restaurant foot traffic. The brief called for showcasing the uniqueness and rich ingredients of the product with maximum appetite appeal while prominently highlighting the competitive price point.

As Creative Lead, I established the core visual look and feel for the campaign, lead-designed primary digital touchpoints, and collaborated with a team of Art Directors and Designers to execute the comprehensive campaign. The integrated ecosystem included a dedicated homepage and campaign landing page redesign, location-geofenced Snapchat filters activated exclusively within IHOP restaurants, Instagram Snap Ads, animated Twitter GIFs, contextual in-navigation and rich media banner ads on Waze and CBS Sports, and promotional email campaigns.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. Core Look & Feel Definition & Website Redesign

- **Visual Direction & Appetite Appeal:** Defined the aesthetic direction, emphasizing coffee-and-pancake ingredient pairings, rich textures, high-impact food photography, and bold price-point messaging.
- **Homepage & LTO Landing Page UX/UI:** Designed updated IHOP.com homepage banners and a dedicated LTO landing page in Photoshop, optimizing product storytelling, ingredient transparency, and nearby restaurant call-to-actions.

### B. In-Restaurant & Location-Geofenced Social Engagement

- **In-Restaurant Snapchat Filters:** Concepted and designed exclusive Snapchat filter overlays that unlocked only via geofencing inside IHOP locations, driving social sharing, framing product equities, and elevating the in-dining experience.
- **Paid Social Assets:** Art directed and created vertical video assets for Instagram Snap Ads and lightweight Twitter GIFs, driving rapid campaign reach across mobile feeds.

### C. Contextual Media Interception & Email Marketing

- **High-Impact Rich Media Banners:** Designed interactive rich media banners deployed across high-traffic platforms including CBS Sports and location-aware ads on Waze to intercept mobile users on the go.
- **Targeted Email Campaign:** Developed high-converting email design templates and InDesign redline specs highlighting the LTO offering and price-point promotion to IHOP's loyalty subscriber base.

## Workflows & Governance
- **Creative Leadership & Collaboration:** Established the overarching creative framework and worked side-by-side with Art Directors and Designers to maintain visual consistency across all touchpoints.
- **Production Spec Hand-off:** Built precise layout redlines, visual asset guidelines, and design specification decks using Photoshop and InDesign to facilitate seamless engineering and media partner hand-off.
- **Commercial & Brand Strategy Integration:** Successfully combined appetite appeal with hard-working price-point messaging across digital touchpoints to stimulate in-restaurant dining.
