## Executive Summary
To modernize IHOP's visual presence and elevate appetite appeal across every guest touchpoint, IHOP initiated a massive, comprehensive refresh of its core food photography. The goal was to replace outdated menu imagery with vibrant, high-impact photography across all print and digital properties.

As Creative Lead, I led the end-to-end creative strategy and execution for this high-volume production initiative. I selected and partnered with the lead photographer, defined visual concepts to secure client buy-in, and directed the month-long photoshoot that successfully produced over 120 standardized, high-quality menu images. The updated imagery refreshed IHOP's in-restaurant print menus, digital ordering platforms, social channels, and national marketing collateral.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. Creative Strategy & Client Buy-In

- **Concept & Style Definition:** Developed comprehensive photography mood boards and visual guidelines framing lighting, composition, prop selection, and food styling to maximize appetite appeal.
- **Client Alignment:** Presented creative concepts and visual directions to IHOP leadership to secure buy-in across print and digital brand standards.

### B. High-Volume Production & On-Set Art Direction

- **Photographer Selection:** Sourced, vetted, and selected the lead commercial photographer and production crew best suited for large-scale CPG/QSR food photography.
- **On-Set Direction (120+ Assets):** Directed an intensive, month-long studio shoot, overseeing styling, lighting, and framing for over 120 unique menu items to ensure consistency across the entire product catalog.

### C. Multi-Channel Deployment

- **Print & Digital Asset Refresh:** Curated and art-directed final retouched assets deployed across in-restaurant print menus, desktop and mobile digital ordering platforms (IHOP.com), social media campaigns, and marketing collateral.
- **Visual Asset Library:** Standardized crop ratios, color profiles, and background specs for developer and agency hand-off.

## Workflows & Governance
- **Production Leadership:** Orchestrated cross-functional collaboration between food stylists, prop stylists, photography crews, and client stakeholders under tight studio timelines.
- **Scalable Visual System:** Established a unified visual photography standard that harmonized physical print menu boards with responsive digital ordering interfaces.
