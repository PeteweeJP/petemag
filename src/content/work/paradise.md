## Executive Summary
To launch IHOP's Limited Time Offer (LTO)—Paradise Pancakes—IHOP needed a high-energy, multi-channel campaign designed to drive in-restaurant foot traffic and highlight the product's tropical flavor profile. The campaign focused on reinforcing IHOP's core equity of "Breakfast All Day" while creating playful appetite appeal.

As Creative Lead, I defined the campaign's visual identity and lead-designed primary digital touchpoints while guiding a team of Art Directors and Designers across channel extensions. I concepted and art-directed a first-in-category, MarCom Gold Award-winning 1.5-hour Facebook Live broadcast filmed three times throughout the day directly on the beach (complete with on-site creative direction and seagull management). The integrated digital ecosystem included a dynamic IHOP.com homepage module that transformed into "Paradise Hour" from 5–8 PM daily, a dedicated "Breakfastarian" brand page, location-geofenced in-restaurant Snapchat filters, Twitter GIFs, Waze navigation ads, CBS Sports rich media banners, and promotional email campaigns.

## Key Initiatives & Strategic Impact
### A. MarCom Gold Award-Winning Facebook Live Experiential Activation

- **First-in-Category Beach Broadcast:** Concepted and art-directed a live broadcast placing Paradise Pancakes directly on the beach, streaming three 1.5-hour sessions throughout the day to visually prove that "it's breakfast all day."
- **On-Set & Broadcast Creative Direction:** Authored broadcast UI graphics, stream overlays, and provided hands-on on-site direction—including managing real-time environmental elements and keeping local seagulls off the prop plates.
- **Award Recognition:** The activation won a prestigious MarCom Gold Award for innovative social media strategy and live brand engagement.

### B. Time-Dynamic Web Ecosystem & "Breakfastarian" Hub

- **"Paradise Hour" Web Personalization:** Designed a dynamic IHOP.com homepage hero module in Photoshop that automatically shifted messaging between 5 PM and 8 PM daily to promote "Paradise Hour" ("Because it's 5 o'clock somewhere").
- **"Breakfastarian" & Product Landing Pages:** Architected a dedicated "Breakfastarian" landing page celebrating all-day breakfast lifestyle imagery alongside product detail pages breaking down ingredient profiles and flavor offerings.

### C. Geofenced Social & Contextual Media Interception

- **In-Restaurant Snapchat Filters:** Designed location-locked Snapchat overlays activated exclusively inside IHOP restaurants to encourage user-generated social sharing while dining.
- **Contextual & Mobile Media Support:** Art-directed Twitter GIFs, Waze in-navigation ad units targeting nearby drivers, high-profile rich media display banners across CBS Sports, and high-converting promotional email blasts.

## Workflows & Governance
- **Integrated Creative Direction:** Established the campaign's visual language and collaborated with Art Directors and Designers to maintain strict aesthetic consistency across web, social, broadcast, and print specs.
- **Developer Hand-Off & Redlines:** Delivered precise Photoshop UI layouts and InDesign redline specification decks ensuring flawless execution across media vendors and frontend developers.
- **Experiential to Conversion Pipeline:** Seamlessly connected a viral social live stream to local foot traffic and digital menu engagement.
