# Peter Magulak

**Creative Director, Experience Design**

## Professional Summary

Creative Director and experience design leader with a track record of award-winning work across the full customer journey — from learn and lead generation through buyflow, marketing campaigns, and core product. Drives design strategy, brand innovation, and digital transformation for Fortune 500 organizations — including Comcast Business, a $10B enterprise division — with deep expertise in UX, design systems, and the rapidly evolving AI landscape. Equally strong as a people leader — builds high-performing teams from the ground up, grows junior designers into senior contributors, and fosters cultures of trust, craft, and accountability that consistently deliver. Fluent in executive storytelling, agile product delivery, and applied agentic AI research.
